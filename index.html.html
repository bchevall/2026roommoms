Party Planner

A repeatable checklist for every classroom celebration so nothing gets forgotten and nobody has to reinvent the wheel.

Before the party

  • Confirm date, time, theme, and teacher approval.
  • Check allergy and food rules.
  • Create volunteer jobs and supply needs.
  • Confirm games, craft, snack, and cleanup plan.
  • Send parent reminder 48–72 hours before.

Day-of checklist

  • Decorations and table setup.
  • Food, drinks, napkins, plates, and utensils.
  • Game/craft supplies and backup activity.
  • Teacher gift or class card, if planned.
  • Trash bags and cleanup crew.

Teacher Appreciation

Keep the class plan coordinated, thoughtful, and easy for parents to participate in at any budget.

Easy class ideas

  • Class card with one note from every student.
  • Favorite drink/snack surprise.
  • Group gift card to a favorite store or restaurant.
  • Flowers, classroom supplies, or wishlist item.

Keep it organized

Post the plan, deadline, contribution instructions, and teacher favorites in one place. Avoid publishing personal payment information on a public website; use your parent group or school-approved tool for that.
